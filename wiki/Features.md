# Module Receipts

# Features

**Receipts** is designed to document and analyze behaviors through a dual lens of accountability and constructive growth.

## 📸 Core Tracking
- **Photo/Video Capture:** Capture timestamped evidence with local-only storage.
- **Categorization:** Organize evidence into custom categories (e.g., "Dishes in Sink", "Laundry on Floor").
- **Positive Tracking:** "Catch them being good" with positive receipts in Littles Mode.
- **Auto-Avatar:** The app automatically updates a person's profile picture based on the category they appear in most frequently ("You are what you do").

## ⚖️ Advanced Mechanics (The Moral Engine)
Instead of paywalls, advanced features are gated by **Redemption Credits** and **Moral Constraints**:

### 🎰 Loot Boxes
- **Mechanic:** Selectively "disappear" embarrassing receipts from your feed.
- **Cost:** Requires **Redemption Credits**, which can only be earned by logging **Positive Receipts** (5 positives = 1 credit).
- **Tiers:** Basic (1 credit), Rare (3 credits), and Legendary (5 credits).

### 🔄 Uno Reverse
- **Mechanic:** If a category threshold is met and an automated summary is triggered, the target can "Uno Reverse" the blast if they have sufficient counter-evidence.
- **Requirement:** Must have tracked more receipts on the initiator than the initiator has on you.

### 🎬 Expose (Hype Videos)
- **Mechanic:** Generate a cinematic compilation of receipts using FFmpeg.
- **Integrity Limits:** 
  - **Threshold:** Requires 50+ receipts in a single category.
  - **Cooldown:** Only one video can be generated every 30 days.

### 🤖 AI Coaching (Littles Mode)
- **Mechanic:** empathetic guidance on communication strategies based on specific logged friction points.
- **Daily Limit:** 3 sessions per day to foster meaningful reflection.

## 🏆 Gamification & Stats
- **Achievements:** 17+ unlockable badges for consistency, patterns of behavior, and positive changes.
- **Evidence Board:** A high-contrast map and tag view of all logged incidents.
- **Group Leaderboards:** Share profiles locally via JSON to see who has the most (or fewest) receipts in your circle.
