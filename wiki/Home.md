## 🚀 The Philosophy: Accountability & Moral Balance
Receipts is more than just a tracking tool; it is a behavioral experiment. It operates on two modes:
1. **Receipts Mode:** For raw, high-accountability tracking.
2. **Littles Mode:** For constructive, wholesome relationship building.

The app is **100% Free and Open Source (FOSS)**. We believe accountability shouldn't be behind a paywall, but it should be governed by ethical constraints. Features like "Loot Boxes" and "Expose Videos" are limited by "Moral Mechanics"—requiring positive actions (Redemption Credits) or time (Cooldowns) to unlock.
